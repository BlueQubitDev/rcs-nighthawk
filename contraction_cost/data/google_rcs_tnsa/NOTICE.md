# Third-party data

The `.graph` and `.groups` files in this folder are the tensor-network descriptions of
five published random-circuit-sampling experiments (Sycamore-53, Zuchongzhi-56,
Zuchongzhi-60, Sycamore-70, Sycamore-67).  They were released with Google's
contraction-order optimizer `rcs_tnsa`,
https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/rcs_tnsa
(Apache License 2.0), and accompany A. Morvan et al., "Phase transitions in random
circuit sampling", Nature 634, 328 (2024).  They are redistributed here unchanged so
that the validation of Appendix E can be reproduced; all rights remain with their authors.
